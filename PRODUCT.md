# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and operations teams manage local and remote hosts, terminal sessions, SFTP transfers, and shared workflows from one desktop application.

## Product Purpose

Voltius is a local-first SSH, SFTP, and serial client that helps individuals and teams connect to hosts, operate terminals, move files, and organize infrastructure without requiring an account or surrendering local data control.

## Brand Personality

Technical, sober, and dependable. The interface should feel precise and operational without becoming intimidating or visually noisy.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-led dashboards, decorative density, inconsistent alignment, low-contrast status text, unexplained motion, and UI patterns that hide operational state behind ornamental cards.

## Design Principles

- Make operational state immediately legible.
- Preserve local-first control and explain remote side effects before they occur.
- Prefer consistent alignment and predictable keyboard behavior over cleverness.
- Keep dense workflows scannable for both individual and team use.
- Treat accessibility, reduced motion, and keyboard navigation as baseline behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, visible focus states, semantic labels, keyboard-complete interactions, and reduced-motion fallbacks. Do not rely on color alone to communicate connection state, source, or errors.
